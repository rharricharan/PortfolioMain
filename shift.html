Shift AI. A debrief coach for physicians, between the hard moments.

Inclusive Health builds support for people under pressure. Shift is its AI debrief coach for physicians, designed with physicians to fit the demands of clinical care: a private space to reflect, reset and move forward, in the few minutes a shift allows.

Designed the product end to end across two shipped versions, MVP through v2, and delivered it dev-ready: onboarding, the home and navigation, Morning Rounds, chats and stories, insights and settings, with every edge case worked through.

The Problem

Physicians and trainees move from one hard moment of clinical work to the next with only minutes in between, and no private place to process what just happened.

Designed With Physicians

The direction came from workshops with Inclusive Health’s leadership: the CEO, CTO and COO, each a physician before founding the company. Their direct medical experience, and how well they know the people Shift is for, shaped the flows and the conviction behind every decision.

Onboarding That Listens

Shift starts by learning where someone is: their school, specialty and current rotation, then what’s pressing on their mind right now. Physicians’ own words set the tone before any feature does, so the first minutes feel like being heard rather than signing up.

Light Enough for a Shift

Physicians have minutes, not hours. Every flow had to be light and doable: open the app, say what happened, get something useful back, and be on your way. Morning Rounds turns that into a ritual: choose a specialty and a case, read the chart, then present it to an attending who listens and pushes back.

Conversations That Listen

Chats come from two directions. A physician can start one whenever they need it, and Shift can open one from a story: a moment from clinical life, like an attending correcting you in front of the team, with a question about how you’d respond. Calm, darker screens for the reflective moments; light ones for getting things done.

Growth, Shown Gently

Insights reflect back what Shift is learning, by area like professional identity and relationships, without turning reflection into a score. Rotations keep it in step with where someone is in training, so prompts and cases follow them from one service to the next.

Decisions

  • Private by design. Nothing about the interface suggests anyone else is watching: no social features, no streak pressure, a space to speak freely.
  • Light and doable. Sessions are short and can be left at any point, so the app fits around a shift instead of competing with it.
  • No guessing at handoff. Rotations, new users, missed rounds and empty states were all designed before handoff, so development never had to guess.

Outcome

The new version has just launched, and it has already contributed to more than 12 institutional partnerships. In the first version’s testing, 4 of 20 participants called the flows “extensive” and the app “confusing”, “complicated” and “frustrating”. After the redesign, 82% of interviewed users found onboarding simpler and quicker to get through, called the experience “very clear” and “focused”, and knew what to do next.

In their words

“Before, I wasn’t sure what the app was doing or where to go. Now it feels like Shift is guiding me and is actively a part of my day-to-day.”
