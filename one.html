Our Next Energy. Batteries that go further, explained for drivers and the grid.

Our Next Energy (ONE) builds energy storage for electric vehicles and the grid. Its Gemini™ dual-chemistry battery significantly extends an EV’s range, and its Aries™ series offers sustainable, durable and efficient storage, built on abundant materials and a strong U.S. supply chain.

Shaped the website’s structure and built its pages on the front end, coordinating content across photography, video and graphics. Beyond the site: the email marketing templates, internal and external presentation decks, and the in-house signage design system, so the brand held together on screen and in the building.

A Site Built Around the Batteries

Battery chemistry is hard to explain. The structure starts with the question people care about, range, then introduces each battery by what it’s for, with its capacity and range right there: Aries for trucks, boats and more, Aries Grid for stationary storage, and Gemini for the longest trips.

Less of the Metals That Cost the Most

Part of the story is what ONE leaves out. The site makes it concrete: next to each chemistry, how much less of the scarce metals it needs.

Aries Grid

For the grid, the page has to work for a different reader: someone buying storage for a factory, a data center or a community. It leads with scale and reliability, then breaks Aries Grid down into its cells, packs and systems.

Beyond the Website

The same system carried into email templates, presentation decks for audiences inside and outside the company, and signage across ONE’s buildings, which became a design system of its own.

Signage for a Battery Factory

ONE’s buildings are labs and production lines, and every room needed a name people could read from down a corridor. The signage started as options: each brand color as a background, Aeonik in regular, medium and bold, and every pairing tested on the same word. Then the room names themselves, sorted by length, from Dry Room to Electrochemical Testing, so a sign’s width follows a rule instead of a guess.

Decisions

  • Lead with range. The site opens on the one question every driver has, then gets technical. Engineers have to scroll a little further; everyone else stays.
  • Built for the team to publish. Pages are built from DatoCMS content, so ONE’s marketing team can add products and news without a developer.
  • One system, on screen and off. Owning the signage alongside the site meant one set of rules from the homepage to the factory floor.

Outcome

The design work and the website’s product strategy shaped how ONE told its story to investors, through Series A and B rounds totaling more than $300M.
