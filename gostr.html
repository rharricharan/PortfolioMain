GoSTR Marketing. Done-for-you marketing that keeps short-term rentals booked.

GoSTR Marketing helps short-term rental owners get more bookings and give guests a better stay. Built on GoHighLevel, it gives each client their own app, text notifications and CRM, so their marketing can grow with their business.

Designed and built the site with the animation and integration behind it, working directly with the co-founders on a site that would be friendly, interactive and informative for owners deciding whether to sign up.

Three Steps, Shown, Not Told

“Unlock your rental’s potential.” The site explains the service as three steps, and each one shows the real thing: the property, the email and text campaign, and the app owners reply from.

  1. Step 1

    Enter your company and property information.

  2. Step 2

    A professionally designed, year-long monthly email and text campaign is built from that information, each one tailored to the brand.

  3. Step 3

    Use the mobile app to respond to re-booking inquiries. It’s that easy.

Pricing Anyone Can Read

“It’s easy to get started.” Pricing sits on the page, by the number of properties, with everything included listed underneath. No call needed to find out what it costs.

Decisions

  • Show the product. Instead of stock photos, each step shows what owners actually get: the emails, the texts and the app.
  • One bold moment, then calm. The holographic gradient carries the brand at the top; everything after is quiet and readable, so the pitch never fights the color.
  • Prices in the open. Showing pricing up front loses a sales conversation, and saves owners the call. For a done-for-you service, that ease is the pitch.

Outcome

After the new site launched, conversions rose 65% and monthly recurring revenue 77%.
