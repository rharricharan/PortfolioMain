01About

I’m a product designer who builds. For nine years, I’ve designed and shipped products and websites for startups and brands, from an AI traffic platform for city engineers to a business phone network built on 5G.

For three years, I ran my own studio, Lumina Studio. Today I’m a Product Designer at A Small Studio® and a member of the Awwwards Young Jury.

If you have an app in mind, I can help you design it, build it and ship it.
