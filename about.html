02How I work

I’ve run a studio of my own, and been the only designer on a founding team. Each asks for something different.

A studio

The work is the system around the work: how projects run, how clients are kept close, and a quality bar that holds on every deliverable.

A team of one

Everything ships through me, from the first interview to the last pixel. No layer to hide behind, and no handoff to wait on.

Lately, small teams that build with AI. How mine works now.

  1. 01

    I build, not only draw

    Designs become real code early, so the thing people react to is the thing that ships.

  2. 02

    Everything’s in git

    Every change is committed. The work has a history, and there’s always a way back.

  3. 03

    Figma to think, code to decide

    Figma is where I explore. Once a direction holds, decisions are made in the browser, on the real thing.

    An idea goes from first sketch to production in days, then keeps improving after launch.

03How I design AI products

I start with the system, build in code and keep the AI honest. The techniques, the rules, the ethics and the tools.

The techniques. What happens before anything gets generated.

  1. 01

    The system comes first

    Type, colour, spacing and components are written down before any screens, so everything the AI makes starts on brand.

  2. 02

    Libraries for the solved parts

    Menus, dialogs and the like come from proven building blocks, so the time goes into what makes the product its own.

  3. 03

    Write it down once

    Anything I’d explain to an AI twice becomes a written rule it follows every time.

  4. 04

    Constraints, not guesses

    The grid, the type scale and every state go in up front, instead of letting the model guess and fixing it after.

  5. 05

    Judge it running

    Ideas are built in code from the start, so decisions are made on the real product, not a picture of it.

The rules. What the AI in my products always does.

  1. 01

    It shows its reasons

    Smartstop’s Nebula logs every decision it makes, so an engineer can always see why traffic changed.

  2. 02

    People keep the final say

    Nebula suggests, and an engineer applies it in one click. Nothing important happens without a person choosing it.

  3. 03

    A private space

    Shift AI is built for confidential reflection: no social features, no streak pressure, nothing that suggests anyone else is watching.

  4. 04

    Light, not demanding

    Sessions are short and can be left at any point, so the product fits around someone’s day instead of competing with it.

The ethics. The lines I hold, in every product.

  1. 01

    Your data stays yours

    People share only what the product needs, and it’s used for them, not around them.

  2. 02

    No pretending

    A suggestion is labelled as a suggestion and a guess as a guess. The AI never claims more than it knows.

  3. 03

    Built with the people it serves

    Physicians shaped Shift AI and traffic engineers shaped STIM. The people living with the product help design it.
